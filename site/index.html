Why Pacifica Sweep

Your mess, your rules.

Digital clutter builds up quietly — duplicate photos, forgotten downloads, sprawling screenshot folders. Cleaning it by hand takes weekends you don't have. Pacifica Sweep does the tedious reviewing for you, then hands every decision back to you.

You stay in charge

Every suggestion waits for your approval. Pacifica Sweep never deletes, archives, or reorganizes anything on its own — every action waits for your explicit approval, whether it runs now or on a schedule you set.

Local-first by design

Your files stay on your machine. Reviewing happens locally, and nothing runs on your device when the app is closed. Your clutter is your business.

Duplicates, found

Identical files and photos are surfaced side by side, so you can keep the best copy and clear the rest — after you decide.

What Pacifica Sweep won't do

  • Won't delete or move anything without your explicit approval.
  • Won't run on your device in the background or while the app is closed. Server-side scheduled tasks you create run only on the schedule you set — and even then, deleting, moving, or archiving waits for your approval.
  • Won't access accounts or folders you haven't chosen to connect.
  • Won't invent a price — plans aren't finalized, and there's no checkout here.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the cleanup automatic?

No. Pacifica Sweep prepares recommendations; every action waits for your approval. There is no mode where it acts on its own.

Does it work while the app is closed?

The app doesn't run while it's closed on your device. If you connect to the platform and schedule maintenance, those tasks run on the server on the schedule you set — each run drafts recommendations, and deleting, moving, or archiving still waits for your approval. You can see and stop them anytime in Tasks.

What can it organize?

Files and photos from sources you explicitly connect. It reviews and recommends — you decide.

How much does it cost?

Pricing isn't finalized yet. There are no charges, trials, or checkout on this site — see the pricing page for the honest status.

Can I try it now?

Pacifica Sweep is in controlled testing. Sign-ups aren't open yet — see the early-access status.

What will I install?

Nothing today — the product is in controlled testing. When early access opens, it'll be a straightforward download that runs on your own machine; your files stay with you.
