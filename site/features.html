Features

Everything in the feature list below exists in the product today. No vaporware, no "coming soon" dressed up as done.

Review Queue

Every suggestion lands in one queue, explained in plain language: what's flagged, why, and what would happen if you approve it. Work through it at your pace — nothing expires, nothing auto-approves.

Duplicate Finder

Identical files and photos are grouped side by side with clear comparisons, so you can confidently keep the best copy and clear the rest. Items that only look similar are flagged as possible duplicates for your review — similar is never treated as identical, and nothing is ever archived automatically.

Batch Recommendations

Related suggestions are bundled into batches you can review together — a whole folder of screenshots, a set of duplicate downloads — with one approval per batch, or item-by-item when you prefer.

Inventory

A complete, searchable view of everything Pacifica Sweep can see: files, folders, and connected sources in one place, so nothing hides in a forgotten corner.

Maintenance Tasks

Recurring tidying — weekly duplicate checks, download-folder sweeps — that you define and start yourself. They prepare recommendations; you still approve every action.

Decision History

A full audit trail of every approval and action: what changed, when, and why. Review your past decisions anytime.

Workspaces

Organize different areas of your digital life separately — personal photos, work documents, shared folders — each with its own review queue and history.

Local-First

The app runs on your machine, with your data staying yours. Nothing runs on your device in the background or while the app is closed.

Roadmap

On the horizon — clearly labeled

The following are planned directions, not current capabilities:

  • Additional cloud-storage integrations
  • Mobile companion apps
  • Shared-household tidy-ups with per-person approvals

We'll move items to the feature list above only when they're built, tested, and verified — not before.
