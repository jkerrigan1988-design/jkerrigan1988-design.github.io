Where things stand

  • The product is being tested with a small controlled group.
  • The features on the features page are what's in the product today — we're inviting people gradually as early access opens.
  • Pricing will be finalized and published before early access opens.
  • When sign-ups open, they'll be announced here first.

Why there's no sign-up form here: we don't have a waitlist system running yet, and a form that pretends to collect your email would be dishonest. When early access opens, this page will have a real sign-up — not a day before.

What early access will look like

When the time comes: a simple sign-up, a straightforward download, and the same deal described on every page of this site — you review, you approve, nothing happens without you. No credit card to "reserve" a spot, no referral hoops.
