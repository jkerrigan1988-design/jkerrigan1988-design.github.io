How Pacifica Sweep Works

A simple loop: you point it at the mess, it does the reviewing, you make the calls. It never acts without your approval.

1

Choose what it reviews

You choose the sources you want reviewed — import a JSON inventory or connect Dropbox. Pacifica Sweep only sees what you explicitly connect — nothing else, ever. You can disconnect a source at any time.

2

It inventories and flags

Pacifica Sweep builds an inventory of what it finds: duplicate files, grouped and explained in plain language in your review queue.

3

Review the recommendations

Each suggestion shows you exactly what would happen — which files are involved, where they'd go, what you'd keep. No jargon, no hidden side effects. Skip anything you're unsure about; it'll wait.

4

You approve, it executes

Only the items you approve are acted on. Every decision is recorded in your history, so you can see exactly what changed and when. Changed your mind? Your trash and archives are yours to restore.

The golden rule: Pacifica Sweep prepares; you decide. There is no automatic mode, no local background worker, and nothing runs on your device while the app is closed.

Staying tidy

Maintenance on your schedule

Once the big cleanup is done, optional maintenance tasks keep things from piling up again — a weekly duplicate check, a monthly download-folder tidy. They run on the schedule you set. Each run refreshes your inventory and drafts recommendations; deleting, moving, or archiving still waits for your explicit approval.
